This notice covers Edenspace Oy's company website and email inquiries. Run of Days has its own privacy information.

Edenspace Oy, Finland (business ID 2656910-7), is responsible for the data it handles. Contact: contact@edenspace.fi.

Visiting this website

We use no cookies, analytics, tracking, or browser local/session storage. Fonts and images are served with the website.

GitHub Pages hosts the site, receives technical request information, and logs IP addresses for security. We use hosting based on our legitimate interest in providing a working, secure website. GitHub determines log retention; its privacy statement explains its processing, international transfers (including to the US), safeguards, and privacy contacts.

When you contact us

We use your email address and message to respond and handle related business matters. The basis is our legitimate interest in handling inquiries, or steps toward a contract at your request. Providing information is voluntary; a reply requires contact details.

Relevant staff and email service providers handle correspondence. We keep it as needed for the inquiry and business relationship, and longer where required by law or necessary for legal claims.

Your rights

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or portability of your data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact us using the address above. We do not profile you or make automated decisions about you.

You may complain to your supervisory authority, including Finland's Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.
