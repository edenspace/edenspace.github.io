Website privacy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

This notice covers Edenspace Oy's company website and inquiries sent to our company contact address. Run of Days has separate privacy information and is not covered by this notice.

Who is responsible?

Edenspace Oy, Finland, business ID 2656910-7, is responsible for the personal data it handles in company inquiries. Contact us at contact@edenspace.fi for privacy questions or requests.

Website hosting

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Loading a page sends your IP address and technical request information to the hosting service. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from visitor analytics; we do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

Our legitimate interest in providing a working, secure website is the basis for using hosting services. GitHub describes its own processing, retention, and international transfers, including processing in the United States and the safeguards it uses, in its privacy statement. See also GitHub Pages data collection. GitHub determines the retention of its security logs.

Cookies and fonts

This company website does not set cookies, store information in your browser's local or session storage, or use third-party tracking. Fonts and images are served with the website, without requests to Google Fonts. Your browser may cache these files for normal page delivery.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and any name or other information you include. We use this information to answer your inquiry and handle related business matters. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in responding to messages and maintaining business relationships, or taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, where applicable.

Providing this information is voluntary, but we need a way to reply if you want a response. Correspondence is handled by the people responsible for the inquiry and the email service providers needed to deliver and store it. We retain it for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any resulting business relationship. Relevant records may be kept longer to meet legal obligations or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in data protection law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, or portability of your personal data, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Send requests concerning data we handle to contact@edenspace.fi. For GitHub's own processing, its privacy statement provides the relevant contact details.

We do not use your data for profiling or automated decisions. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority, including Finland's Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman.
