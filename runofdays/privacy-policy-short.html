Privacy - Run of Days (Short)

Last updated: 20 April 2026

Data used

  • Apple Health running workouts, heart rate, VO2max, workout route/elevation data, and optional profile values, only with your permission
  • Local app settings, milestone state, and widget snapshot data stored on your device
  • Anonymized diagnostics such as crashes and performance reports provided by Apple

What we do not do

  • No user accounts
  • No third-party analytics or ads
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No Health data sent to Edenspace Oy servers
