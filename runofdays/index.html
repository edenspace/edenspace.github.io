Support and FAQ
For support, email runofdays@edenspace.fi. Include your device model and iOS version, and what you expected vs what happened.
How do I enable Health access
Open the app and tap Request Access. If you previously denied access, enable it in iPhone or iPad Settings, then Health, then Data Access and Devices, then Run of Days, and enable permissions.
Why do I see No runs even though I ran
Run of Days reads runs from Apple Health. Please check that your run exists in the Health app under Workouts, and that Health permissions are enabled for Run of Days. Then try pull to refresh or restart the app.
How are streaks calculated
A streak is a sequence of consecutive calendar days with at least one running workout per day. Multiple runs on the same day count as the same day in the streak.
Purchases and billing
Run of Days offers a one time purchase to unlock Streak Details. Purchases are handled by Apple. If features do not unlock, open the in app purchase screen and use Restore Purchases. You can also reinstall the app and restore your purchase on the same Apple ID.
