How do I enable Health access?
Open Run of Days and tap Request Access. If you previously denied access, open iPhone Settings, then Health, then Data Access and Devices, then Run of Days, and enable the requested permissions.
Why do I see no runs?
Run of Days reads running workouts from Apple Health. Check that the workout exists in the Health app, confirm Health permissions are enabled for Run of Days, then pull to refresh or restart the app.
How are streaks calculated?
A streak is a sequence of consecutive calendar days with at least one running workout per day. Multiple runs on the same day count as one streak day.
What are heart-rate zones?
Run of Days uses an approximate five-zone model. It uses your max heart rate if you set one, otherwise it can estimate max HR from year born and sex. Resting heart rate can be used for heart-rate reserve when available.
What is the difference between VDOT and VO2max?
VO2max is imported from Apple Health when a same-day sample is available. VDOT is estimated by Run of Days from a run's recorded distance and duration. Both are estimates for orientation, not medical or coaching-grade output.
What counts as a record?
Records track personal bests across longest run, fastest pace, highest average heart rate, and longest duration. Each is tracked both all-time and within the current streak, and a celebration appears when a new record is set.
Can I export my data?
Yes. The app can export a selected streak as CSV and can also export every recorded run as CSV from Settings.
