Privacy Policy — Run of Days

Last updated: 3 January 2026

Summary

  • The app reads running and VO₂max data from Apple Health only with your permission.
  • Health data is processed locally on your device.
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs are used.
  • Apple provides anonymized diagnostics data (crashes/performance).
  • Subscriptions are handled by Apple via the App Store.

1. Who we are

Run of Days (“the App”) is developed and operated by Edenspace Oy, a Finnish limited liability company (“we”, “us”).

Contact: runofdays@edenspace.fi

2. Health data (Apple HealthKit)

With your explicit permission, the App may read:

  • Running workouts (time, duration, distance, pace, speed)
  • Heart rate summaries (when available)
  • VO₂max samples (when available)

This data is used solely to display streaks, summaries, and charts in the App. We do not store Health data on our own servers, and we do not share it with third parties.

3. Diagnostics and performance

Apple may collect anonymized or aggregated crash and performance diagnostics and make them available to developers through App Store Connect and Xcode. This data is used only to improve app stability and performance.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Purchases are processed by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive your payment card details. Apple may provide subscription status so the App can unlock features.

5. Data we do not collect

  • No user accounts
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No precise location data
  • No third-party tracking

6. Data retention

  • Health data remains on your device and in Apple Health
  • Diagnostics retention is handled by Apple

7. Your rights

You can manage Health data permissions via iOS settings. EU/EEA users have rights under GDPR; most controls are exercised through Apple and device settings.

8. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated; the “Last updated” date will reflect changes.

10. Contact

Email: runofdays@edenspace.fi

Apple’s processing of purchases and diagnostics is governed by Apple’s own privacy policies.
